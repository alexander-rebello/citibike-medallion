Limits of this data

  • Rider types are defined differently before and after 2019, so the casual share is not comparable across that line.
  • Stations were added mostly outside the centre. A falling share of trips in the centre reflects the growing network as well as changing habits.
  • Before 2020 the data has no bike type, so those trips are counted as classic bikes and the e-bike share for those years is zero by assumption.
  • 2026 covers January to October only.
  • Trips shorter than one minute or longer than 24 hours, depot and test stations, and duplicate records are removed (0.09 % of raw rows).
